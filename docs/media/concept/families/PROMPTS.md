# Prompts pendientes (misma cabecera para todos)

Cabecera común:
> Original creature character for a mobile pet-raising game, painted digital illustration (rich painterly rendering, soft rim light, glossy highlights, subtle texture, professional game concept art, NOT childish, NOT chibi). Full body, strict side view facing RIGHT, standing on four legs, whole creature visible, centred, on a plain flat light grey background with no scenery, no shadow on the ground, no text.

Final común:
> Clear readable silhouette, separated limbs (legs and tail not overlapping the body) so it can be cut into parts and rigged for 2D animation.

- **Ferrón (metal):** an armoured dog-like creature of the metal family. Brushed blue-grey steel plates with copper joints and rivets, a glowing cyan visor across the eyes, sturdy legs with metal paws, a short segmented metal tail, small exhaust vents on the back emitting warm amber sparks, loyal disciplined posture.
- **Prismino (cristal):** a lynx-like creature of the crystal family. Deep indigo-violet body made of translucent faceted crystal with an inner light, shards of glowing cyan crystal along the back that refract tiny rainbows, crystal tufts on the ears, slender legs, a short faceted tail, almond-shaped bright white eyes with a thin ring pupil, elegant cool expression.
- **Umbrardo (sombra, flota):** a wolf-like creature of the ghost/darkness family that floats slightly above the ground. Dark violet-black smoky fur, front legs solid while the hind body dissolves into drifting purple mist (no hind legs), a hood of shadow over the head, glowing lavender-white eyes without pupils, a wispy smoke tail, faint purple glow.
- **Aletín (agua):** an axolotl-dragon of the water family. Glossy wet deep-blue skin with emissive cyan tips, six feathery gills around the head, a flat paddle-shaped tail with a fin, webbed feet, small fins along the back, almond-shaped pale-cyan luminous eyes, a few water bubbles, curious expression.
- **Gelatón (gelatina):** a land jellyfish creature of the gel family. A translucent teal jelly bell-shaped body with a glowing warm-yellow core and small bubbles inside, standing on four springy jelly legs, a short wobbly tentacle tail, almond-shaped white eyes, greedy cheerful expression.
- **Velisto (aire, flota):** a spectral fox of the ether/air family. Soft periwinkle-blue fur that becomes transparent toward the edges, three long flowing tails of wind with pale-gold glowing tips, long ears, small feathered tufts on the shoulders, a faint halo, white luminous eyes, wise mischievous expression.
- **Escorial (escama):** a chameleon-like creature of the earth/scale family. Earthy brown and moss-green scales with golden-ochre accents, a bony helmet crest, turret-like independent eyes with amber slit pupils, a curled prehensile tail, strong clawed feet, observant calm expression.
